maybe you're reading this across the planet or maybe i'm your neighbour, forse siamo diversi o forse siamo la stessa cosa. When i look around i can see walls (even if my room is quite messy) and so do you. And even if you can't… now you're thinking about walls, seeing them.

I'm not that fluent in english but sometimes i feel the urge to write even in this language… i'll also try to translate as many things as i can from italian to english so i can give them a new life, why not.

Per quanto riguarda l'italiano beh… la maggior parte delle cose che ho scritto in vita mia sono in italiano e non vedo l'ora di condividerle ora che sono riuscito a ritagliarmi un piccolo spazio digitale!
